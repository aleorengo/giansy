# Piccoli pensieri and request cart

Ready-made bundles are the category next to Biscotti senza tempo. One pouch with 3 small cookies: 4.50 EUR; one bag with 12 small/medium cookies: 15 EUR; one brass tray with 15 small/medium cookies: 20 EUR. Packaging is included. The quantity means complete packages, not individual cookies. Plus/minus immediately updates the original “La tua dolce selezione” summary; there is no bundle add-to-cart button and no second cart section. Bundles never appear on the silver plate and never consume a personalised builder's capacity.

The current custom assortment is included directly in the final request. Customers can optionally save it to repeat it or compose another; completed custom assortments are snapshotted into the shared summary (including packaging and all product quantities). Identical assortments merge; other assortments stay separate. Repeating an assortment multiplies its complete contents and packaging fee. Cart state is saved separately in browser storage; drafts remain independent. The request form collects a required non-past desired date, name, email and prominent personalisation notes. Date availability remains to confirm; final submission is still an explicitly labelled unsent preview while the recipient is provisional. No payment or external delivery has been added.

## Image outputs

Built-in image_gen editing mode, no CLI/API fallback. PNG masters: `assets/bundles/little-pouch.png`, `sweet-bag.png`, `brass-gift-tray.png`. Site exports: `public/images/bundles/*.webp`. 800 × 800 pixels, true alpha, product fitted within 760 × 760 then padded. Sharp only trims transparent space, resizes/pads and exports. Source cellophane reflections and translucent surfaces are AI-assisted reconstructions; these are illustrative clean cutouts, not pixel-exact manual masks. The photographed products illustrate the presentation; the supplied bundle piece counts and prices define the catalogue.
